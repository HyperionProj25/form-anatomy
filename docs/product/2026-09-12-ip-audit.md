# IP audit v1: what is ours, what is licensed, what must change before a commercial deal

12 September 2026. Internal. Covers the code, the model, the data, the text,
and the fonts in the Form repository as of commit `274580b`.

## 1. Code

- **Application code** (`src/`, `scripts/`, `tests/`): written in this
  project; no copied third-party source. Ownership: Baseline Analytics (to be
  confirmed by the contributor agreements Chase holds with anyone who has
  committed). Licence in the repo: none declared yet. Action: add a licence
  file (proprietary for the product; MIT is fine for the atlas if Chase wants
  it open) so the position is explicit.
- **Runtime dependencies** (shipped to users): react, react-dom (MIT), three
  (MIT), lucide-react (ISC). All permissive; no copyleft.
- **Build and test dependencies** (not shipped): MIT except draco3d and
  typescript (Apache-2.0) and axe-core (MPL-2.0, used only in tests; MPL's
  file-level copyleft does not reach our code). All compatible with a
  proprietary product.
- Transitive dependencies: 390 packages. Action before contract: run a full
  licence scan (`npx license-checker --production --summary`) and attach the
  report; a GPL surprise deep in the tree is the thing to rule out.

## 2. The anatomy model

- `public/body.glb`: Z-Anatomy (Gauthier Kervyn and contributors), built on
  BodyParts3D (DBCLS), adapted for the browser by hpfrei. **Licence: CC
  BY-SA 4.0.**
- What that means: attribution is required and works derived from the model
  must be shared under the same licence. For the free atlas this is met
  (credits in the app, model downloadable unchanged). For a commercial
  product that TrackMan owns or licenses exclusively, share-alike is a
  problem: the posed and re-skinned model in the swing lab is arguably a
  derivative, and an exclusive licence cannot be granted over CC BY-SA
  material.
- `src/data/geometry.json` (joint pivots, muscle-to-bone contact points)
  and `public/skin` weights are derived from the model's vertices: treat as
  derivative of the model.
- **Action, blocking for the deal:** license a commercially clean
  musculoskeletal model with a rig, or commission one. Candidates to price:
  Zygote (Zygote Body / 3D male musculoskeletal), Anatomography commercial
  terms (DBCLS), Elsevier 3D4Medical (Complete Anatomy assets, usually not
  licensable), TurboSquid or CGTrader anatomy rigs with an extended commercial
  licence, or a commissioned build by a medical 3D artist against the
  catalog's 826 parts. The catalog, attachments, joints, pivots and pipeline
  are model-agnostic in code; swapping the model is a build-geometry rerun
  plus visual QA.

## 3. Data

- **TrackMan sample** (`DataSampleBaseline`, four sessions): supplied to
  Baseline Analytics as a sample. Derived, anonymised joint-angle curves from
  eight swings and the four session summaries are in the repository and the
  public site at Chase's direction. **Action:** written confirmation from
  TrackMan of what may be published; under a deal, production data is
  contractual and this sample is replaced.
- **CMU subject 124** swing: CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, free,
  commercial use permitted with attribution, no resale of the data. Only
  derived curves are in the repo. Clean.
- **Wikipedia-derived text** (structure facts, Latin names, descriptions):
  CC BY-SA 4.0. Fine in the free atlas with attribution (present). In a
  commercial product either keep the attribution and share-alike for that
  text (it is separable content, not code) or replace it with licensed or
  original text. Not blocking; flag it.
- **Wikidata Latin labels**: CC0. Clean.
- **Research citations** (`src/data/research.ts`): bibliographic facts and
  our own summaries; no copied abstracts. Clean.
- **Study sets, lessons, applied notes, caveat copy**: written here. Ours.

## 4. Fonts and icons

- Fonts: system font stack; no bundled font files. Clean.
- Icons: lucide-react (ISC). Clean.
- `public/og.png`, `favicon.svg`, icons: made here. Ours.

## 5. Names

- "Form" is a common word; as a product name it is weak and likely
  conflicts in software classes. **Action:** trademark clearance search
  before any contract names it; consider a distinctive product name for the
  hitting product (the atlas can keep "Form").
