# Form pilot guide

A twenty-minute session for a small group of students, on their own laptops or
phones, at https://hyperionproj25.github.io/form-anatomy/. Watch where they
hesitate; that is the feedback.

## Before the session

- Ask each student to open the link once on the wifi they will use, so the
  8 MB model is cached before the session starts.
- Have the "Report a problem" link in the footer in mind; anything that
  confuses two people is worth a report.

## Tasks (about four minutes each)

1. **Find a structure.** Find the soleus. Then find it a second way (search
   box, region filter, or clicking the model). Which was faster?
2. **Run a study set.** On the start panel, open "Rotator cuff". Step through
   all four with the arrow keys. Turn on "Show on model" in the attachments
   block for one of them.
3. **Take a quiz.** "Test your knowledge", pick a region you are studying, and
   finish ten questions. Look at the weak spots it records.
4. **Follow a fascial line.** Switch to Fascia, choose the superficial back
   line, start the tour, and read one evidence badge. Say in one sentence what
   the badge means.
5. **Take something away.** From any playlist, open the handout and either
   print it or download the Anki deck.

## Questions to ask afterwards

1. What did you try to do that the tool would not let you do?
2. Which name, label or badge did you not understand?
3. What would you use this for in the week before a practical exam?
4. On a phone, what was too small or too slow?

## What to send back

The four answers per student, plus any screenshots of confusion. The footer
link opens the issue tracker; one issue per problem is ideal.
